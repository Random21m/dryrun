DRYRUNPractise on a pretend SeedSigner before a real one holds your money.

SELF-TEST RUNNING

DRYRUN is an unofficial practice tool, not made by or connected to SeedSigner. The real project is at seedsigner.com (code: github.com/SeedSigner/seedsigner). This page works offline, tracks nothing and stores no seed.

Open the device below and try everything: make a seed from dice, back it up, load it, export the xpub, sign a practice transaction. Lessons below the device walk you through each step. Only use seeds here that you will never put money on.

Dice, coin and card check

Type the same dice rolls you gave the device (Tools > New seed > Dice rolls). You should see the same words. If you do not, the device may be fake or tampered with: do not use it. Do this on an offline computer, then CLEAR ALL.

Backup check

Type your words back in from your paper, in order, to see the fingerprint and the first addresses. They must match what the device shows. A list number must sit on the word at its place; the page never puts words in another order.

Printable templates

Blank sheets to write your words on, or to draw a SeedQR by hand. They print on A4 or Letter. Print them before you make the seed; never print a seed.

The order of steps

  1. Practise here first, until every step feels easy.
  2. Print a blank word sheet (PRINT) before you start.
  3. On the real SeedSigner, make a new seed from your own dice rolls (Tools > New seed > Dice rolls).
  4. Write the words on paper, in order, with their numbers. Check each one twice.
  5. Do the device's own backup test (it asks you some of the words).
  6. Check the dice: type the same rolls into CHECK on an offline computer. The words must match.
  7. Write down the fingerprint. Export the xpub to your wallet app and look at the first address.
  8. Test the backup before you put money on it (below).

What never to do

  • Never take a photo of your words or your SeedQR.
  • Never type your words on a phone or on a computer that is online.
  • Never store them in a password manager, an email, a note app or the cloud.
  • Never tell anyone your words. No real support person will ask for them.
  • Never put real money on a seed you made or typed on this page.

Test a backup before you put money on it

  1. Turn the SeedSigner off and on, so it forgets the seed.
  2. Load the seed again from your paper only (or scan your SeedQR).
  3. The fingerprint must be the one you wrote down. If it is not, a word is wrong: fix the paper first.
  4. Send a small amount first. Then load the seed from paper again and check you can sign a spend of it.

You can also type your words into CHECK, on an offline computer, to compare the fingerprint and first address. Then press CLEAR ALL and close the page.
