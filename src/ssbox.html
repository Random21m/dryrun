Turn the practice device on or offA copy of SeedSigner 0.8.7: the same screens, menus and buttons. Closing this switches it off and wipes it.

Click the buttons on the case, or click the device and use the keyboard: arrow keys move, Enter or Space presses the joystick, 1, 2 and 3 are KEY1, KEY2 and KEY3. It runs inside this page only. CLEAR ALL or closing it wipes it.

Lessons

Short lessons that walk you through the device, one press at a time. Each step moves on by itself when the device shows the right screen. They work with the seed loaded on the device, real or practice; with none loaded, they load the public test seed abandon ... about (anyone can make it, so never put coins on it). Their practice transactions spend made-up coins, so they can never be broadcast.

Practice transaction

The practice wallet: sample transactions that spend made-up coins of the seed on the device, held up to the device’s camera. Two of them have a problem to spot: do not sign those.
